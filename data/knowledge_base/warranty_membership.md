# Warranty, Membership & Other Services

## Product warranty
All electronics come with the manufacturer's warranty, usually 1 year from delivery; the exact length is listed on each product page. Warranty covers manufacturing defects, not accidental damage, water damage or normal wear. To make a claim within the first 30 days, contact us and we handle it. After 30 days, contact the manufacturer directly using the details in the product manual; we can help you find them.

## Extended protection plans
At checkout you can add a 2-year or 3-year protection plan to eligible electronics. Plans cover accidental damage and can be cancelled within 30 days for a full refund.

## ShopEase Plus membership
ShopEase Plus costs $49 per year and includes free express shipping on all orders, early access to sales, and a 45-day return window. You can cancel any time in My Account > Membership; if you haven't used any benefit you get a full refund within 14 days of joining.

## Gift cards
Gift cards are available from $10 to $500, never expire, and can be used on any product except other gift cards. Lost gift card codes can be re-sent to the purchaser's email.

## Gift wrapping and messages
Gift wrapping is available for $3.99 per item and can be selected at checkout. Gift receipts hide prices and allow the recipient to return the item for store credit.

## Customer support hours
Live chat with a human agent is available Monday to Saturday, 8 AM to 8 PM. Tickets opened outside these hours are answered within 24 hours.
