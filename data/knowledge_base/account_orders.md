# Account & Order Management

## Cancelling an order
You can cancel an order yourself in My Account > Orders while its status is "Processing". Once the status is "Shipped" it can no longer be cancelled - instead, refuse the delivery or return it after it arrives under our returns policy. Cancelled orders are refunded in full.

## Modifying an order
Items can't be added to an order after it has been placed. To change items, cancel the order while it is still "Processing" and place a new one.

## Order statuses explained
- Processing: payment authorised, the order is being prepared.
- Shipped: handed to the courier, tracking available.
- Out for delivery: with the local driver today.
- Delivered: the courier marked the package as delivered.
- Cancelled: the order was cancelled and any charge is being refunded.

## Resetting your password
Click "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and enter your account email. The reset link is valid for 1 hour. If you don't get the email within 10 minutes, check your spam folder. Support agents can never see or ask for your password.

## Changing your email or phone number
Go to My Account > Profile. Changing your email requires confirming a code sent to both the old and new address.

## Deleting your account
You can request account deletion under My Account > Privacy. Your account is deleted within 30 days; order records required for tax purposes are kept as required by law.

## Guest checkout
You can order without an account. To track a guest order, use the order id and email from your confirmation email.

## Account security
If you notice an order you didn't place, change your password immediately and contact support so we can stop the shipment and secure your account.
