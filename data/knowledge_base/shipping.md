# Shipping & Delivery

## Shipping options and costs
We offer three shipping options within the country:
- Standard shipping: 4-7 business days. Free on orders of $50 or more, otherwise $4.99.
- Express shipping: 2-3 business days, $9.99.
- Next-day delivery: order before 2 PM local time on a business day, $19.99. Not available for remote areas or oversized items.

## Processing time
Orders are packed and handed to the courier within 1 business day. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are processed the next business day. Pre-order items ship on the release date shown on the product page.

## International shipping
We ship to over 40 countries. International delivery takes 7-15 business days and costs are calculated at checkout based on weight and destination. Customs duties and import taxes are the customer's responsibility and are collected by the courier on delivery.

## Tracking your order
As soon as your order ships you will receive an email with a tracking number and a link to the courier's tracking page. You can also see tracking under My Account > Orders. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan.

## Changing the delivery address
You can change the delivery address in My Account > Orders while the order status is "Processing". Once an order has shipped the address cannot be changed by us, but you can contact the courier with your tracking number to request a redirect.

## Late, lost or missing packages
If your package is more than 5 business days past the estimated delivery date, or tracking shows "Delivered" but you haven't received it, check with neighbours and your building reception first, then contact support. We will open an investigation with the courier, which takes up to 3 business days. If the package is confirmed lost we send a free replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer.
