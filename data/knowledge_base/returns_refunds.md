# Returns & Refunds

## Return window
Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, with all tags and accessories. Electronics that have been opened can be returned within 15 days if they are in like-new condition.

## Non-returnable items
The following cannot be returned unless they arrived damaged or defective: gift cards, downloadable software, personal care and beauty products that have been opened, underwear and swimwear, perishable food, and personalised or custom-made items.

## How to start a return
1. Go to My Account > Orders and select the order.
2. Click "Return items", choose the items and a reason.
3. Print the prepaid return label (or show the QR code at a drop-off point).
4. Drop the package off within 7 days of creating the return.
Returns are free for damaged, defective or wrong items. For change-of-mind returns a $5.95 return shipping fee is deducted from the refund.

## Refund timelines
Once the warehouse receives your return, we inspect it within 2 business days and issue the refund to the original payment method. Card refunds take 5-10 business days to appear on your statement depending on your bank. PayPal refunds take 1-3 business days. Store credit is applied instantly. Original shipping fees are refunded only if the return is due to our error.

## Exchanges
We don't do direct exchanges. Return the original item for a refund and place a new order for the size or colour you want. This is the fastest way to get the new item.

## Damaged, defective or wrong items
If an item arrives damaged, defective or isn't what you ordered, report it within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the item and packaging. We will send a free replacement or issue a full refund including shipping, and you won't need to pay for the return.
