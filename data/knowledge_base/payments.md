# Payments & Billing

## Accepted payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, store gift cards, and Buy Now Pay Later through Klarna (orders between $50 and $1,000). Cash on delivery is not available.

## When you are charged
Your payment method is authorised when you place the order and charged when the order ships. If an order is split into several shipments, you may see separate charges for each shipment.

## Payment declined
If your payment is declined, check that the billing address matches the one your bank has on file, the card hasn't expired, and you have enough available balance. Some banks block online purchases until you confirm them in their app. We cannot see the reason for a decline - please contact your bank.

## Duplicate or unexpected charges
A pending authorisation for a cancelled or failed order is released automatically by your bank within 3-7 business days. If you see two completed charges for the same order, contact support with your order id and we will investigate within 2 business days.

## Promo codes and discounts
Only one promo code can be used per order, and codes can't be applied after the order is placed. Promo codes can't be combined with clearance items unless the code says so. If a code isn't working, check its expiry date and minimum spend.

## Price adjustments
If the price of an item you bought drops within 7 days of your order, we refund the difference as store credit. Request it through support with your order id. Price adjustments don't apply to flash sales or clearance prices.

## Invoices
A tax invoice is emailed when your order ships and can be downloaded any time from My Account > Orders > Invoice.
